# Borrower FAQ

*Fictional policy of First Community Bank — synthetic content for the LoanLens AI demo.*

## How do I check the status of my application?

Log in to the loan portal and open your application. The status line shows one of: Submitted, Needs Review, Approved, or Rejected. "Needs Review" means a loan officer is looking at an item our automated checks flagged — you do not need to do anything unless we ask for a document.

## Why was my document rejected?

The most common reasons are: the photo was blurry or had glare, the document was expired, the date was older than 30 days (for pay stubs and bank statements), or the name on it did not match the application. Re-upload a clear photo of a current document.

## How long does a decision take?

Auto loans: within 2 business days of a complete application. Personal loans: within 1 business day. The clock starts when the last required document passes verification, not when the application is first submitted.

## Can I update my application after submitting?

Yes. If a document shows different information than your application (for example, a different income figure), you can correct the application to match the documentation. Corrections restart verification for the affected fields only.

## I'm applying for a small business loan — why was my business name flagged?

The legal or DBA name on one of your documents doesn't exactly match the business name on your application — even a small difference like "&" versus "and" counts. This is a routine check, not a rejection; a loan officer will confirm which spelling is correct before your application moves forward.

## Why was my requested loan amount flagged?

The amount you requested is well above what our rough, automated affordability estimate suggests is supportable by your documented income (or, for a business loan, documented annual revenue). This is not a denial — it's a routine check that asks a loan officer to take a closer look, since it could reflect other income sources, existing savings, or simply a typo in the amount entered.

## Who sees my documents?

Documents are processed automatically to extract and verify key fields, then reviewed only by the loan officer assigned to your application. Decisions are always made by a person, not by the automated system.
