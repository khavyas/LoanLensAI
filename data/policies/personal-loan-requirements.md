# Personal Loan Requirements

*Fictional policy of First Community Bank — synthetic content for the LoanLens AI demo.*

## Eligibility

Personal loans from $2,000 to $25,000 are available to applicants 18 or older with verifiable income. No collateral is required. Funds may be used for any lawful personal purpose except investment securities or gambling.

## Required documents

Every personal loan application requires: (1) a recent pay stub dated within the last 30 days, (2) a valid driver's license or passport, and (3) the most recent monthly bank statement. Applicants who receive income from multiple employers should provide one pay stub per employer.

## Income requirements

Minimum documented gross monthly income is $2,500. Total monthly debt obligations including the new loan payment may not exceed 45% of gross monthly income. Stated income must match documented income within 5%.

As a rough affordability estimate (not a credit decision), the system compares the requested amount against documented monthly income assuming a 45% payment ratio over a 36-month term, capped at the $25,000 product maximum. A requested amount above this estimate is flagged for officer review, not automatically declined — it may simply mean the applicant has other income or an unusually short desired term.

## Decision timeline

Complete applications receive a decision within 1 business day. Incomplete applications are held for up to 30 days awaiting documents, after which they expire and must be resubmitted.
