# Application Process and Timelines

*Fictional policy of First Community Bank — synthetic content for the LoanLens AI demo.*

## Step 1 — Apply

Complete the online application with your personal details, employer, gross monthly income, and requested amount. You will receive a confirmation immediately.

## Step 2 — Upload documents

Upload each required document (see the Document Checklist). Documents are verified as you upload them, and the portal shows exactly which items are still outstanding at all times.

## Step 3 — Verification

Extracted information is cross-checked against your application. If everything matches, your application moves straight to decisioning. If something does not match, the specific item is flagged, you are told why, and a loan officer reviews it.

## Step 4 — Decision

A loan officer issues the final decision within the product's decision timeline (1 business day for personal loans, 2 for auto loans, measured from the moment your file is complete). You are notified in the portal and by email.

## Expiration

Applications with outstanding documents expire after 30 days. Expired applications must be resubmitted from the beginning.
