# Auto Loan Requirements

*Fictional policy of First Community Bank — synthetic content for the LoanLens AI demo.*

## Eligibility

Applicants must be 18 or older, hold a valid government-issued ID, and have verifiable monthly income. Auto loans are available for new and used vehicles up to 8 years old. The maximum loan amount is $75,000. The requested amount may not exceed 110% of the vehicle's book value.

## Required documents

Every auto loan application requires: (1) a recent pay stub dated within the last 30 days, (2) a valid driver's license, and (3) the most recent monthly bank statement. Self-employed applicants substitute the pay stub with their two most recent tax returns.

## Income requirements

Documented gross monthly income must be at least 3 times the estimated monthly loan payment. Income stated on the application must match documented income within 5%; larger differences require additional proof of income or a corrected application before underwriting can proceed.

As a rough affordability estimate (not a credit decision), the system compares the requested amount against documented monthly income assuming a 3x payment ratio over the maximum 72-month term, capped at the $75,000 product maximum. A requested amount above this estimate is flagged for officer review — it does not mean the loan is denied, and it may be a data-entry error rather than a genuine affordability issue.

## Interest rates and terms

Terms range from 24 to 72 months. Rates are set at decision time based on credit profile; the demo assistant must never quote a specific rate — applicants are referred to a loan officer for current rates.

## Decision timeline

Complete applications receive a decision within 2 business days. Applications missing documents are placed on hold and the applicant is notified of exactly which items are outstanding.
