# Income Verification Policy

*Fictional policy of First Community Bank — synthetic content for the LoanLens AI demo.*

## Verification standard

Income entered on the application must be supported by documentation. The bank compares stated gross monthly income against the documented amount from pay stubs or tax returns. Differences of 5% or less are accepted as rounding. Differences greater than 5% trigger a verification exception.

## What happens on an income mismatch

The applicant is notified which figure did not match and by how much, and may either correct the application to match the documentation or upload additional proof of income (for example, a second pay stub or an employer letter). An application with an unresolved income exception cannot proceed to underwriting.

## Converting pay frequencies

Weekly pay is multiplied by 52 and divided by 12. Bi-weekly pay is multiplied by 26 and divided by 12. Semi-monthly pay is multiplied by 2. Annual salary is divided by 12. All comparisons use gross (pre-tax) amounts.

## Human review guarantee

Automated checks flag exceptions; they never decline an application. Every flagged application is reviewed by a loan officer before any decision is made.
