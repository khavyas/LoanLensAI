# Late Payment and Grace Period

*Fictional policy of First Community Bank — synthetic content for the LoanLens AI demo.*

## Late payment grace period

The grace period is 10 days. A payment is not considered late until 10 days after its due date. A payment received within the grace period is not charged a late fee and is not reported as late.

## Late fee

If a payment is received after the grace period has ended, a late fee of $25 is added to that payment. The fee is charged once for each missed payment.

## If you expect to miss a payment

Borrowers who expect to miss a payment should contact their loan officer before the due date. Hardship arrangements are reviewed case by case and are not guaranteed.
